# Executive Summary

Knowledge Gravity is one of HXAI Research's core technologies.

It is responsible for making the Knowledge Universe feel alive.

Instead of displaying a static graph of connected concepts, Knowledge Gravity continuously reorganizes the user's research environment based on curiosity, relationships, context, relevance, and scientific importance.

Every research session has its own center of gravity.

As the user's interests change, the universe changes with them.

Knowledge is not stored as isolated pages.

Knowledge behaves like a living universe.

• Community interest (future)
